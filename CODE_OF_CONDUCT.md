# Community Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming space for contributors, including people making their first contribution. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, trolling, intimidation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption after a request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Reporting

**Maintainer action required before inviting contributions:** Replace `[CODE_OF_CONDUCT_REPORTING_CONTACT]` with a monitored private email address or private reporting form. If the report concerns a maintainer, provide an independent alternative: `[ALTERNATE_REPORTING_CONTACT]`.

Send a private report with what happened, where and when it happened, relevant links or screenshots, and your preferred way to be contacted. Share only information needed to investigate. Avoid posting reports about individuals in public issues. If anyone faces immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

Maintainers will review reports as privately as practical, listen to affected people, and avoid retaliation. They may ask for more context and limit information sharing to people who need it to respond. They cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may give a private warning, require a change in behavior, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban someone from project spaces. They may also decline to act when a report is unsupported or outside project scope. Reporters and subjects should be informed of outcomes when practical, without exposing another person's private details.

The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is an optional recognized standard maintainers may choose to adopt separately. This policy does not claim to be a particular version of it.
